## How to use this book

Study the learning units in order or follow the coverage map to target a gap. Related outcomes may be combined into one teaching section or split across sections when that makes the concepts clearer. Use explanations, examples or hands-on activities where relevant, exam applications, and self-check questions; then use mapped mock-exam questions to test recall and application. Revisit weak areas before attempting a timed exam.

The official study guide defines scope, not a complete course. Product details can change; version-sensitive points are marked for verification and should be checked against linked Microsoft documentation.
