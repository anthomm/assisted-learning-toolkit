## Extraction and organization notes

The source’s five weighted domains became five chapters. The skill groups were combined into 15 book topics to make related tasks teachable together; this organization is a pedagogical choice, not an official Microsoft outline. Outcomes in `coverage.md` are interpreted in original wording, with internal stable IDs. The source guide scopes this study aid but does not guarantee exhaustive exam coverage.

The chapters emphasize decision-making and operational consequences as well as portal and command-line workflows. Product limits, supported configurations, service behavior, and portal labels can change. The book links to Microsoft Learn and marks details that should be rechecked before implementation. Any explanatory material beyond a stated skill is enrichment, not a claim that it is an exam objective.

Content is original and is not Microsoft material. The source is linked rather than reproduced. All material is drafted for edition 1 and has not yet had a human technical review.
